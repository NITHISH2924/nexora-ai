Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA & Google Play User Data Policies

1. Introduction

NEXORA AI ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android mobile application and web platform collect, use, store, and protect your information.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Your email address and cryptographically hashed password when you create an account.
  • User Generated Content: Conversations, text prompts, uploaded files (documents, images), project notes, and custom AI personalization preferences.
  • Audio Data: Temporary microphone audio when using speech-to-text features, processed strictly for voice input transcription.
  • Technical & Device Data: IP address, user-agent string, and timestamps for security audit logging, brute-force attack prevention, and session management.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, personalize, and maintain the NEXORA AI multi-modal assistant services.
  • To authenticate your identity and protect against unauthorized access.
  • To execute requested AI actions including reasoning, document analysis, image generation, and code generation.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce platform security policies.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Providers

NEXORA AI connects securely to enterprise AI inference providers (such as Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or local neural engines). Your prompt data is transmitted solely for real-time inference. We do not sell your personal data or user content to third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 / HTTPS). Authentication tokens are securely signed (JWT) and stored locally using Android hardware-backed KeyStore and EncryptedSharedPreferences. Database records enforce strict multi-tenant tenant isolation.

6. Permissions Requested in Android App

  • Internet: Required to communicate with the secure backend API.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Optional, requested only when you tap the voice dictation button.
  • Camera / Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Optional, requested only when uploading photos or documents for analysis.
  • Notifications: Optional, for long-running AI task completion alerts.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may delete individual chats, files, images, or memories at any time directly in the application. To permanently delete your entire account and all associated data, you can use the in-app Settings > Delete Account flow or visit our Account Deletion Request Page.
